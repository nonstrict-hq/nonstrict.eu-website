Privacy Policy

Last updated June 4, 2026

This document for Capture Inspector from Nonstrict B.V. describes how and why we might collect, store, use and share ("process") your information when using our applications or services.

This policy applies when you download and use our application Capture Inspector, or any other application of ours that links to this privacy policy.

Goal

Nonstrict tries to handle your data as carefully as possible. No data is stored, other than for the correct functioning of the application.

Data usage

Your preferences are stored on your devices, they are not shared with Nonstrict or any other third party.

Analytics

We collect basic aggregate product interaction data, which is not linked to individual users.

This includes, but is not limited to: the number of app launches, hardware and software version numbers, and errors that occur. No content your devices capture (such as microphone audio, camera images, screen captures) is collected.

Captured content

Capture Inspector uses capture capabilities of macOS for devices such as, but not limited to camera, microphone and screen.

These permissions are used solely to read and display technical information (such as supported formats, resolution, frame rate, and input levels) about the devices connected to your Mac. Or to capture their content at your request.

Captured content is shown only on your Mac and is never transmitted off your device by Capture Inspector.
