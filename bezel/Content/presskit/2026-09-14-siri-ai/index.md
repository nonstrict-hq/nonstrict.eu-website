### Ready for Siri AI

**Give a screenshot a polished look.** Open a screenshot in Bezel and just say which frame and background you want.

![](siri-white-frame-wallpaper.png)

**Prepare a screenshot for social media.** Open a screenshot of your new feature in Bezel and ask Siri to make it square, on a background that stands out.

![](siri-square-pink.png)

**Send it to a colleague.** Siri sees the screenshot you're looking at, so you can pass it on without saving or attaching anything.

![](siri-email.png)

Bezel's actions are also available in the Shortcuts app on Mac, iPhone and iPad. For example, to frame a whole batch of App Store screenshots at once.

### A screenshot editor on the Mac

Bezel for Mac now has a full editor for your screenshots and screen recordings.

![](mac-editor.png)

**Share progress with your team.** Take a screenshot or recording while mirroring your iPhone, and it's already framed. Drag it straight into Slack, an email or your release notes.

**Make screenshots for your website or App Store page.** Drop in screenshots from your iPhone or the Simulator, pick a background and a size, and export.

**Put a demo in your presentation.** Trim a screen recording down to the part that matters and export it as a framed video for Keynote.

### Framed videos from screen recordings

Bezel already turned screenshots into framed shots. Now it does the same for screen recordings.

![](framed-screen-recordings.png)

**Show off your app on social media.** Record your screen, frame the recording in Bezel, and export it square or in portrait, ready to post.

**Show what you mean.** Turn a quick screen recording of a new feature or a bug into a clean, framed clip your team or your users instantly understand.

### Availability

The new version is available on September 14, 2026, together with the release of iOS 27 and macOS 27. Using Bezel with Siri requires a Mac, iPhone or iPad that supports Siri AI, running macOS 27 or iOS 27.
