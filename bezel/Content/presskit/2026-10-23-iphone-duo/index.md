Apps on iPhone Duo adapt to how the device is held. Bezel gives anyone a quick way to show how apps look folded, unfolded or anywhere in between, from just a screenshot. Available on Mac, iPhone and iPad. In September, Apple featured Bezel on the App Store in "Great Apps Updated for iOS 27".

### Frame iPhone Duo screenshots in every pose

#### Stills

Drop in a screenshot or screen recording and pick a pose. In seconds, the app sits on an iPhone Duo held exactly the way you held it, ready to share in a review, a pitch or on social media. No mockup templates, no photo editing.

#### Shorts

Take one screenshot of the outer screen and one of the inner screen, and Bezel turns them into a short: a video of iPhone Duo folding open, with the app changing from one screen to the other. It's the clearest way to show how an app carries over when the phone unfolds, ready in seconds and with no video editing. Two frames from a Figma mockup work just as well.

Bezel has two more shorts available, one showing the phone being folded partially into a book pose, the other when you set the device down half open like a small laptop.

![A short made with Bezel: iPhone Duo folding open, created from just two screenshots.](duo-unfold.mp4)

### The Bezel iPhone app itself also adapts to every pose

With the Bezel iPhone app, you can frame and share a screenshot right on your phone, no Mac needed. Background, pose, padding and size are each a tap away.

On iPhone Duo, the Bezel app adapts to every pose. Folded, it works as on any iPhone. Unfolded, the tools move to both edges of the larger screen to stay easy to use. Open the sidebar to control everything with one hand. Bezel's tools also adapt to iPhone Duo being partially folded, aligning with and moving out of the fold.

The iPhone Duo update is a free update, available on October 23, the day iPhone Duo arrives. Press can try the update now on TestFlight.
