iPhone Duo test

Thanks for helping test the iPhone Duo compatibility of the Bezel Mac app! We want to learn if live mirroring works with both a cable and AirPlay.

Before you begin

Make sure you have the latest version of Bezel for Mac.

Download Bezel

Version 5.4 or later. The free version is fine.

Step 1

Mirror using a cable

  1. 1

    Open Bezel, click Mirror iPhone or iPad… and choose Cable. Follow the steps until the iPhone shows on the Mac.

  2. 2

    When the iPhone appears:

    {{ partial "page/duo-test-moves.html" . }}
  3. 3

    Unplug the cable. Keep Bezel open: quitting it clears the logs.

Step 2

Mirror using AirPlay

  1. 1

    On the iPhone, open Control Center › Screen Mirroring and pick Bezel: ‹your Mac›

    Not the plain Mac name, that one skips Bezel.

  2. 2

    When the iPhone appears:

    {{ partial "page/duo-test-moves.html" . }}
  3. 3

    Stop mirroring in Control Center.

Step 3

Send us the logs and your feedback

The log shows us where we can improve. And we’d love to hear if mirroring worked well for you.

  1. 1

    In Bezel, choose Help › Gather Customer Support Logs… and save the file.

  2. 2

    Please email us the log file and tell us how it went.
